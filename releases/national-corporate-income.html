National Income

National Income Statement — Quarterly

Statements are shown newest to oldest. Levels are $ millions at seasonally adjusted annual rates (SAAR); costs appear in parentheses as a display convention.

Quarterly National EBITDA is shown as EBITDA (Calculated), defined as Gross Profit minus Total Operating Expenses. The National statement displays the effect of BEA’s source-signed Statistical Discrepancy as an adjustment so the EBITDA-to-EBIT bridge is explicit. Annual EBITDA is a separate construction.
