Versioning

Current data and Statistical Releases

Current Data

The six datasets on this page form the current canonical EARL data product. They may change when later official source releases are incorporated and processed.

Statistical Releases

Published Statistical Releases preserve version-specific publication snapshots and records. They are a separate publication layer and do not replace the current Data product.
