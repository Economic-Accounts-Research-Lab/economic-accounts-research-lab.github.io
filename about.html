About

Economic Accounts Research Lab

EARL translates official U.S. economic-account data into a business-facing framework designed to make the structure and performance of the economy easier to interpret, analyze, and explore.

The project connects National, Corporate, and Industry perspectives with transparent methodology, reproducible construction, downloadable data, and documented statistical-release practices.

Citation & use

Project & methodology citation

Cite EARL's methodological framework

When referring to EARL as a research project or methodological framework, cite the Methodology Paper:

Economic Accounts Research Lab (2026), EARL Methodology: Constructing Business-Style Economic Accounts from U.S. Economic Data, Version 1.0, October 2026.

Release & dataset citation

Cite the version actually used

When using data, results, or a publication snapshot, cite the specific dataset or Statistical Release together with its date or version and public link.

Use & interpretation

Economic analogues, not company-accounting identities

EARL is an analytical presentation of official economic-account data, not a collection or consolidation of firm-level financial statements. Business-facing labels are intended to aid interpretation; they do not assert formal equivalence to GAAP, IFRS, tax-accounting, or company-reporting definitions.

  2. 2ValidateEconomic, accounting, data, and technical QA.
  3. 3FreezeFix publication inputs, metadata, and version.
  4. 4ApproveHuman review and sign-off.
  5. 5PublishRelease and preserve the public artifact.
How published numbers can change

Source revision

The underlying official source data changed.

Methodology change

EARL changed a mapping, construction, or calculation.

EARL correction

A previously published EARL output contained an error.

Published release artifacts are preserved so that changes can be distinguished rather than silently overwriting prior publication states.
